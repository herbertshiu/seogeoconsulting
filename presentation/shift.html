The search surface
has expanded.

Search still rewards pages people can find, understand, and trust.

Generative answer surfaces add a second requirement: useful passages need to be retrievable, attributable, and clear enough to cite.

Knowledge System over Links

Move from ranking individual pages to building a coherent, connected knowledge system.

Proof over Publishing

Move from publishing high-volume content to proving specific, authoritative claims.

Citation over Position

Move from position reporting to measuring qualified discovery and citation rates.
