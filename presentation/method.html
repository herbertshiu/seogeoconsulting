The four-move
authority system.

01

Define the entity

Make the organisation, audience, geography, offer, and point of view unambiguous for both people and machines.

02

Answer the intent

Lead with a direct answer to real questions, then support it with context, examples, and logical next steps.

03

Prove the claim

Use first-hand observations, methodology, citations, and outcomes to give readers and engines something to trust.

04

Measure the mention

Look beyond rank. Track qualified discovery, branded demand, and the pages or queries that earn AI citations.
