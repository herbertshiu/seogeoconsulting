**SEO / GEO authority building strategy**

## Slide 2 — The search surface has expanded
Search still rewards pages people can find, understand, and trust. Generative answer surfaces add a second requirement: useful passages need to be retrievable, attributable, and clear enough to cite.

**The strategic shift**
- From ranking pages to building a coherent knowledge system
- From publishing more to proving the right things
- From position reporting to measuring qualified discovery and citation

## Slide 3 — The four-move authority system
**01 — Define the entity**
Make the organisation, audience, geography, offer, and point of view unambiguous.

**02 — Answer the intent**
Lead with a direct answer, then support it with context, examples, and next steps.

**03 — Prove the claim**
Show authorship, methodology, first-hand detail, primary sources, and limitations.

**04 — Measure the mention**
Track discovery, engagement, conversion, and the pages or queries that earn citations.

## Slide 4 — Case study: iTE Limited
**Public-source case study · Hong Kong / Greater Bay Area**

The challenge was not a lack of capability. iTE Limited presents strategy, media, social, SEO, platform development, and technical consultancy. The authority opportunity is to help each audience understand how the offer, geography, and organisation connect.

**Authority pattern**
Entity clarity → offer architecture → evidence trail → source-of-truth links

This is a public-source analysis, not a claim of private performance data.

## Slide 5 — Proof should be visible
**The case study evidence layer**

- Public service detail supports the relationship between strategy, media, SEO, and technology.
- Contact and location details make the organisation legible as a real local entity.

## Slide 6 — Benchmark table: experience + retrieval
**Reference benchmarks, not performance claims**

| Signal | Reference | Strategic use |
|---|---:|---|
| LCP | ≤ 2.5 sec | Loading performance baseline |
| INP | < 200 ms | Responsiveness baseline |
| CLS | < 0.1 | Visual stability baseline |
| AI citations | Establish a baseline | Track how often pages are cited |
| Grounding queries | Track recurring phrases | Learn which questions retrieve your content |

Google Search Central defines the Core Web Vitals thresholds. Microsoft Bing’s AI Performance guidance defines citation, cited-page, grounding-query, and trend reporting as useful AI visibility signals.

## Slide 8 — What to build next
**90-day authority operating rhythm**

**Days 1–30:** consolidate entity language, technical baselines, indexability, canonical URLs, and priority intent pages.

**Days 31–60:** publish evidence-led pages, strengthen internal links, add complete structured data, and create one case study with approved proof.

**Days 61–90:** review qualified organic demand, conversions, cited pages, grounding queries, and content freshness. Iterate from evidence.

## Slide 9 — Measurement is the compounding loop
Discover → understand → trust → act → return

The durable advantage is not a single ranking or a single citation. It is a system that keeps making the organisation easier to understand across people, crawlers, and answer engines.
